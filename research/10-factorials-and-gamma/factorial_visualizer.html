Is it Continuous?

The graph clearly shows that the function is **not continuous** at n = 0. There is a "jump" or a gap in the line.

  • As 'n' approaches 0 from the positive side (the blue line), the value approaches 1.
  • Based on a continuous function that fits your negative integer points (the red line), the value also approaches 1 as 'n' approaches 0 from the negative side.
  • Your requirement for the limit from the negative side to be -1 would introduce another discontinuity. The function shown is the closest continuous representation for negative values that matches your integer factorial definitions like `(-3)! = -6`.
