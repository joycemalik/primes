# Changelog

## v1.0.0, 19 October 2026

The first public release.

### The film
- *Where the Waves Go Quiet* at [primes.joycemalik.com](https://primes.joycemalik.com): an opening, six acts, 13 scenes and an epilogue, narrated in first person and in handwriting.
- Live soundtrack generated in the browser: the prime pulse, the waves of small primes as a chord, the prime-walk melody, the zeta zeros as tones, and Robin's riser.
- Every figure computed live, including ζ(s) itself (checked against mpmath to 8 digits).
- Epilogue with every original claim re-tested, a Robin's-inequality tester, further reading, a searchable archive of the research files, and contact details.
- Search and sharing metadata: sitemap, robots.txt, Open Graph and structured data.

### Packages
- `@joycemalik/primewaves` (JavaScript, GitHub Packages) and `primewaves` (Python, wheel on the release). Both include the sieve, the prime wave, Z(n), complex ζ and Γ, the first 1,000 zeta zeros, the explicit formula, Robin's criterion and Cipolla's estimate.

### Research
- About 170 scripts, 15 notebooks and 1,100+ figures from June to September 2025, organised into ten topic folders in the order they were explored. Each folder has a README and each script a header comment giving its original name and purpose. See REORGANIZATION.md.
